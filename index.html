FAQ

Questions patients ask

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Tell us when you book. We'll take things slowly, explain each step and agree on a "stop" signal before we begin.

Do you take emergency patients?

Yes. We keep slots free every day for pain, swelling and broken teeth. Call us first thing and we'll fit you in.

Do you accept insurance?

We work with most major plans and can check your cover before your visit.

How do I change my appointment?

Use the link in your confirmation email, or call us. Changes are free up to 24 hours before.
